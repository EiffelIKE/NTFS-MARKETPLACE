The first bullet is the script's standard text for local audits; for this report the pages were fetched from the Vercel deployment instead (HTTPS, CDN), with no local build or server.

- Build: `pnpm build` (Vite production build, minified, hashed assets), served by `vite preview` on `http://localhost:4173` (HTTP/1.1, gzip, no CDN).
